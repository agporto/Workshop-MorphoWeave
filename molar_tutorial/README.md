# Molar data

110 training and 5 held-out (`test/`) second lower molars of prosimian primates and close relatives, each with 16 expert landmarks. The empty folders are where the [tutorial](../README.md) writes its results.

## Source

This is the teeth dataset from:

> Boyer, D. M., Lipman, Y., St. Clair, E., Puente, J., Patel, B. A., Funkhouser, T., Jernvall, J., & Daubechies, I. (2011). Algorithms to automatically quantify the geometric similarity of anatomical surfaces. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 108(45), 18221–18226. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1112822108

The data was collected and organized by Doug M. Boyer and publicly distributed with the CPsurfcomp suite (version 1.0, 2011) by Yaron Lipman, via [arXiv:1110.3649](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.3649) and the [Weizmann Institute](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~ylipman/CPsurfcomp/). The original scans are on [MorphoSource](https://www.morphosource.org/projects/000000C73). The copies here were aligned in MorphoWeave, which is why their names end in `_sas_align`. One specimen of the original 116 is not included.
